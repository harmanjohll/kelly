How ordering works

Choose a piece, or let the consultation help. Your brief goes to Kelly on WhatsApp. Kelly will reply to confirm your order and send a PayNow request.

Fresh flowers are delivered on Friday and Saturday; order by Wednesday, 6pm. Each piece is made to order, usually within 5 to 10 days.

The price Kelly quotes is the price you pay, delivery included. No countdown timers. Your recipient's details are used only to deliver their flowers.
