Fleeting

Fresh flowers, chosen at the market for each delivery day and tied by hand. A week of colour and scent, then they're gone, which is part of why they move people.

Lasts
About a week
Care
Fresh water every two days
Best for
Celebrations, romance, a table at home

Forever

Flowers crocheted stitch by stitch from cotton yarn. They need no water, carry no pollen, and stay exactly as they arrived.

Lasts
As long as you keep it
Care
A gentle dust now and then
Best for
Hospital wards, allergies, keepsakes, gifts sent from afar

A fresh bouquet with one crocheted bloom tucked inside. When the fresh flowers fade, one remains.

Careful with meaning.

  1. Four stems are set aside for Chinese families, because four (四) sounds like the word for death.

  2. Lilies stay out of homes with cats, and pollen stays off hospital wards.

  3. White chrysanthemums are kept for mourning, never for a birthday.

How ordering works

Choose a piece, or let the consultation help. Your brief goes to Kelly on WhatsApp. Kelly will reply to confirm your order and send a PayNow request.

Fresh flowers are delivered on Friday and Saturday; order by Wednesday, 6pm. Each piece is made to order, usually within 5 to 10 days.

Promises

The price Kelly quotes is the price you pay, delivery included. No countdown timers. Your recipient's details are used only to deliver their flowers.
