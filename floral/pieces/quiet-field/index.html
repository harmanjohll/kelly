What the flowers say

Chrysanthemum

Chinese A classical symbol of autumn and long life, but white and yellow chrysanthemums are used for mourning, so they are kept away from celebrations.

Japanese The imperial flower. White chrysanthemums are strongly linked with funerals.

Victorian Cheerfulness; white for truth.

Lisianthus

Victorian Appreciation; a later addition to the flower language, so sources vary.

Eucalyptus

Today A modern florist's green, chosen for its soft scent and silver colour rather than an old meaning.
