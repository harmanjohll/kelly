The One That Stays

Fresh, with one crochet bloom

A fresh hand tied bouquet with one crochet bloom tucked inside. When the fresh flowers fade, one remains.

This is the piece that holds both halves of what I make. Enjoy the fresh flowers for their week, then keep the crocheted bloom for as long as you like.

How it lives

Lasts
Fresh stems for about a week. The crochet bloom stays.
Care
Trim the stems and change the water every two days. Lift the crochet bloom out before the vase is emptied.
Size
About 40cm tall, held in two hands.

What the flowers say

Spray rose

Victorian A smaller cousin of the rose, read as affection and gentleness.

Lisianthus

Victorian Appreciation; a later addition to the flower language, so sources vary.

Eucalyptus

Today A modern florist's green, chosen for its soft scent and silver colour rather than an old meaning.

Rose

Victorian Love; red for passion, pink for grace and gratitude, white for innocence.

Japanese Red roses carry romantic love (ai, koi) in hanakotoba.

Chinese Red is the colour of joy and good fortune, which makes red roses a natural fit for romance and celebration.
